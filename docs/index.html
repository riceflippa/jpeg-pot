MULTICHAIN PROTOCOL PROTOTYPE

NFTs enter.
Licensing funds the pool.

Lucky Commons gives idle NFTs prize eligibility. Rights-cleared media can earn onchain licensing revenue without selling the underlying NFT.

THE PRODUCT

One loop. Two users.

01

Holders deposit

An NFT becomes a pool position on its source chain.

02

Buyers license

Verified media is licensed with native crypto.

03

The pool earns

Revenue can fund prizes, drops, reserves, or $LUCK buy-and-burn.

Rights stay explicit. Pool membership does not require commercial media rights.
