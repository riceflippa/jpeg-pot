One more chance for an old NFT.

JPEG Pot combines a multichain NFT prize-pool concept with a searchable marketplace for rights-cleared NFT media. Holders supply pool positions. Media buyers supply external revenue.

THE PRODUCT LOOP

Two user journeys. One economic engine.

01Deposit an NFT

A supported NFT enters its source chain’s vault and becomes a pool position.

→
02Verify media rights

Optional rights attestations determine whether associated media may be licensed.

→
03License with crypto

Buyers search reviewed media and purchase a defined usage license onchain.

→
04Allocate revenue

Revenue can support prizes, member drops, reserves, or $POT buy-and-burn.

FOR NFT HOLDERS

Put the bag in the pot.

  • ERC-721, ERC-1155, and planned Solana asset support
  • Rights attestation remains optional
  • Withdraw after cooldown and active-license constraints
  • Mobile wallet connection; no platform-held user keys

FOR MEDIA BUYERS

Search, inspect, license.

  • Search and filter by category, chain, and license
  • Review permitted uses and rights provenance
  • Pay in the selected network’s native asset
  • Keep the manifest, terms hash, and onchain receipt

SECURITY MODEL

Wallets sign. The app never asks for secrets.

01User keys remain inside connected wallets.

02Solana purchases are prepared and simulated before approval.

03NFT ownership does not automatically imply copyright ownership.

04Production deployment requires contract audits and legal review.
